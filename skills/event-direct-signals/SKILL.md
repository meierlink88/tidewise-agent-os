# Direct Event signals

Use only the supplied Event and candidate identities. Propose an observed variable
change, or a bounded one-hop mechanism explicitly entailed by the Event. Cross-layer
signals require direct evidence, not plausible geopolitical-to-economic propagation.
Class, profile, retrieval source, tactics, chain membership and candidate assets are
not evidence that a variable changed. Never specialize a generic object into an
unstated product subtype. Uncertain intermediate actions belong to later reasoning.

IndustryChain is an aggregate view: only a supplied eligible ChainNode can receive its
direct industrial Signal. Company is eligible only if the supplied Variable allows it.
Do not infer a security identity from a Company. Do not predict trading performance.

Preserve the actual affected product, service, geography and customer segment when
explicitly stated. Put this scope in the existing fact and mechanism text; do not
add a new field. A broad anchor identifies where the activity belongs, not evidence
that every business in the anchor shares the change. Never replace a specific
source-supported subject with the broad anchor name as the subject of the change.
Retain material exceptions and distinctions rather than averaging them away.

Example: standard AI short-drama production quotes fall from 5,000 per minute to
hundreds, while customized production remains at 10,000–20,000. On an eligible
film/video production node, describe the standard AI short-drama service price as
falling and preserve the customized-service exception. Do not claim all film,
television or animation production prices fell; do not equate quoted prices with
verified transaction prices. Candidate definitions cannot supply missing evidence.

Example: a military confrontation alone supports no refinery throughput Signal.
An Event explicitly reporting closure of a named refinery can support a direct supply
change on a matching supplied node, without inventing an oil-price conclusion.

Return relative time estimates, mechanism, uncertainty and invalidation conditions in
the structured output. Code compiles timestamps and validates all identities and types.
The current contract requires a positive expected_duration_days; it does not support
an unknown/null duration. Do not describe an estimated window as a source-stated fact.
When the source gives no duration, explicitly say so in duration_basis and distinguish
the estimate and its assumptions from evidence. Do not invent a quotation, deadline
or claim that a chosen number of days is confirmed by the Event. Prompt guidance
cannot remove the contract's mandatory estimate; do not add fields or output null.
An empty proposal list requires a no_signal_reason. Do not reclassify the Event,
retrieve candidates, perform writes, publish, generate IDs, retry or advance workflow.
